# Decision desk image briefs

These are prompts for you to generate and return assets, not claims that images have been generated. No image service was invoked. The current interface needs no images to function. Keep financial labels, amounts and charts as accessible HTML, never embedded in artwork.

## Shared visual direction

Premium, restrained editorial 3D illustration for Pishbin, an Iranian SME financial planning product. Warm off-white background, deep ink/navy forms, teal accents, soft diffuse daylight, subtle paper and matte ceramic textures. Clean geometry, generous negative space, consistent camera and lighting. No lettering, numbers, logos, bank marks, flags, currency symbols, watermarks or screenshots. No exaggerated upward arrows, guaranteed-profit symbolism or US dollar imagery. These are decorative metaphors, not representations of actual customer data.

## 1. Main decision desk hero

Create a landscape 3:2 editorial illustration following the shared visual direction. On the left third, a small business desk with three abstract ledger cards, a compact calendar and a translucent teal path joining them into one clear sequence. A modest bridge represents planning ahead, not investment returns. Keep the right half nearly empty for right-aligned Persian HTML heading overlays. Calm, practical, trustworthy; no people or text. Deliver approximately 1536×1024 with room to crop to a wide banner.

## 2. Cash calendar

Create a square illustration following the shared visual direction: a low tabletop calendar made of blank matte tiles, with three teal markers and one amber marker. A simple continuous path connects upcoming dates. No numbers, text or chart axes. Communicate timing of commitments, not alarm or guaranteed safety. Center the object with generous margins, suitable for a compact educational or empty-state card. Deliver approximately 1024×1024.

## 3. Decision simulation

Create a square illustration following the shared visual direction: one neutral origin tile splitting into two gently diverging paths, with a small blank purchase card beside one path. Both paths remain plausible; neither uses trophy, growth arrow or winner imagery. Emphasize comparing choices rather than predicting the future. No text or numerical values. Deliver approximately 1024×1024.

## 4. Collections follow-up

Create a square illustration following the shared visual direction: three blank invoice cards arranged in a tidy priority stack beside a small unopened envelope and a muted amber reminder marker. Respectful and human, not aggressive debt collection. No phone screen, send indicator, customer identity or legible invoice data. The image must not imply a message has been sent. Deliver approximately 1024×1024.

## 5. Customer behavior

Create a square illustration following the shared visual direction: three abstract customer tokens beside small neutral timeline tiles of different lengths. Show variation in payment timing without ranking people as good or bad. No credit score, stars, badges, protected-trait cues, portrait faces or official verification symbols. Deliver approximately 1024×1024.

## 6. Data readiness

Create a square illustration following the shared visual direction: a magnifying lens hovering over aligned blank ledger cards, with one small gap being carefully aligned. A subtle teal accent indicates review in progress, not certification or completed bank reconciliation. No shield, government seal, text or checkmark implying guaranteed correctness. Deliver approximately 1024×1024.

## Returning assets

Return the hero plus any selected supporting images, identifying which brief each matches. Prefer WebP or PNG; retain original resolution. Review for unintended text, irrelevant money symbols, excessive contrast and cropping before integration. Use decorative images with empty alt text when neighboring HTML already conveys their meaning; do not replace tables, status labels or interactive controls with images. Assets are not integrated until supplied and reviewed.
